Common Questions

MERCEDES OIL CHANGE FAQ

How often does a Mercedes need an oil change?

Mercedes ASSYST PLUS monitors driving conditions and notifies you when an oil change is due — typically every 10,000–15,000 miles. However, for turbocharged engines and AMG models we recommend a 7,500-mile interval to protect against oil degradation and carbon buildup in high-performance applications.

What oil does my Mercedes need?

Mercedes-Benz requires full synthetic oil meeting MB 229.5, 229.51, or 229.52 specification depending on your engine. AMG models often require a specific viscosity and additive package. We confirm the correct spec for your exact VIN before we start — never a one-size-fits-all approach.

Do I need to go to a Mercedes dealer for oil changes?

No. Any shop with Mercedes-trained technicians, STAR-compatible diagnostic tools to reset the ASSYST service indicator, and MB-spec oil can properly service your Mercedes — for significantly less than dealer labor rates. That's exactly what we offer.

Will the oil change reset my service light?

Yes — but only with a proper ASSYST PLUS reset using STAR-compatible diagnostic software. Many general shops skip this step entirely or can't perform it without the right tools. We reset the service indicator on every Mercedes oil service as standard.

Do you service all Mercedes models?

Yes. We service the full Mercedes-Benz lineup including C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, GLC, GLE, GLS, CLA, CLS, SL, G-Wagen, and the full AMG performance lineup.

How long does a Mercedes oil change take?

A standard Mercedes oil change with multi-point inspection and ASSYST reset typically takes 45 minutes to an hour at our shop.
