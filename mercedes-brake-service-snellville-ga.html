What You Get

COMPLETE MERCEDES
BRAKE SERVICE

From a routine pad replacement to a full brake system overhaul, we handle every component of your Mercedes-Benz braking system.

🔴
Brake Pad Replacement
We replace front and rear brake pads with OEM or OEM-equivalent compounds matched to your Mercedes model — preserving the pedal feel and stopping performance Mercedes engineered in.
⭕
Rotor Resurfacing & Replacement
Warped or worn rotors cause vibration and reduced stopping power. We measure rotor thickness and surface condition, then resurface or replace as needed.
🔧
Caliper Inspection & Service
Sticky or seized calipers cause uneven brake wear and pull. We inspect, clean, and lubricate caliper slides on every brake job, and replace calipers when necessary.
💧
Brake Fluid Flush
Mercedes recommends a brake fluid flush every 2 years. Moisture-contaminated fluid lowers its boiling point and can cause brake fade. We flush and replace with the correct DOT spec fluid.
📡
Brake Wear Sensor Replacement
Most Mercedes models use electronic brake wear sensors. We replace sensors with new pads so your warning system stays accurate.
🖥️
Brake System Diagnostic Scan
We use Mercedes-compatible diagnostic software to scan for ABS, ESP, and brake system fault codes — catching electronic issues a visual inspection alone would miss.

It depends heavily on how you drive. Most Mercedes owners see 30,000–70,000 miles from a set of pads, with front pads wearing faster than rears. Your car will tell you before it becomes urgent — Mercedes uses electronic wear sensors that trigger a dashboard message when the pads reach their limit.

Because the wear sensor was almost certainly not replaced. Mercedes brake wear sensors are consumable — once the pad wears down to them, the sensor circuit is destroyed and a new pad alone will not clear the message. We fit new sensors with every pad replacement and reset the service message before the car leaves.
