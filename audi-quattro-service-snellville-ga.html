AUDI QUATTRO
SERVICES WE OFFER

The Quattro system is one of the most capable AWD systems ever built — but only when properly maintained. We service every component of the Quattro drivetrain.

01
Quattro System Diagnostic
We scan for AWD, Haldex, and drivetrain fault codes using VCDS and OEM Audi diagnostic software — identifying issues with the Haldex controller, torque vectoring, and differential operation before symptoms become failures.
02
Haldex Fluid & Filter Service
The Haldex rear differential on transverse-engine Quattro vehicles (Golf R, TT, A3, Q3) requires a fluid and filter change every 25,000–40,000 miles. Neglecting this is the leading cause of Haldex pump failure — a very expensive repair.
03
Rear Differential Fluid Service
Longitudinal Quattro vehicles (A4, A6, A8, Q5, Q7) use a rear differential that requires periodic fluid changes. We use the correct Audi-spec gear oil to protect the limited-slip or open differential internals.
04
Center Differential Service
Audi's Torsen center differential on longitudinal Quattro vehicles is virtually maintenance-free but can develop issues on high-mileage vehicles. We inspect and service the center diff as part of a full Quattro drivetrain check.
05
Driveshaft & CV Joint Service
Worn CV joints and driveshaft vibrations are common on high-mileage Quattro vehicles. We inspect driveshaft boots, CV joints, and center support bearings — replacing worn components before they fail and leave you stranded.
06
Haldex Pump Replacement
A failing Haldex pump causes loss of rear-wheel drive engagement and the Quattro system effectively becomes front-wheel drive. We replace Haldex pumps with OEM units and perform the required adaptation reset after installation.

Common Questions

AUDI QUATTRO SERVICE FAQ

What is the difference between Haldex and Torsen Quattro?

Haldex Quattro is used on transverse-engine Audi models (A3, TT, Q3, and Golf R platform vehicles). It uses an electronically controlled clutch pack to send power to the rear wheels on demand. Torsen Quattro is used on longitudinal-engine models (A4, A6, A8, Q5, Q7) and uses a mechanical torsen differential that continuously distributes torque. Both need periodic maintenance — but the service procedures and parts are completely different.

How often should I service my Haldex system?

Audi recommends Haldex fluid and filter changes every 25,000–40,000 miles depending on the generation. In practice, many owners skip this service entirely, which leads to Haldex pump failure. A pump replacement is a $600–$1,200 repair that's almost entirely preventable with regular fluid changes.

How do I know if my Quattro system isn't working properly?

Signs of a Quattro system issue include an AWD warning light, the car behaving like front-wheel drive in slippery conditions, vibration or clunking during tight low-speed turns, or a VCDS fault code related to the Haldex controller or rear differential. If you notice any of these, bring it in before the system fails completely.

Can a failing Haldex system damage my Audi?

Yes. A Haldex pump that fails while the system is trying to engage can damage the clutch pack and internal components. More commonly, a neglected Haldex system simply stops engaging the rear wheels — turning your Quattro into a front-wheel-drive car without any warning. Regular fluid service is cheap insurance.

Do you service Audi RS and S models with Quattro?

Yes. We service the full Audi performance lineup including S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S8, RS3, RS4, RS5, RS6, RS7, and the TT RS. Performance Quattro variants often have sport differentials and torque vectoring systems that require specific diagnostic software and fluid specs — we have both.

What does a Quattro service typically involve?

For Haldex vehicles: fluid and filter change, Haldex controller scan, and pump operation test. For Torsen vehicles: rear differential fluid change, center differential inspection, and driveshaft/CV joint check. We always start with a diagnostic scan to check for stored fault codes before recommending any fluid services.
