What You Get

BMW TRANSMISSION
SERVICES

From a targeted repair to a full rebuild — we handle every level of BMW transmission work.

🔍
Transmission Diagnostic
We use BMW-compatible ISTA diagnostic software to pull fault codes, live data, and shift quality readings — pinpointing the exact cause before any parts are ordered.
🛢️
Fluid Service & Pan Gasket
BMW automatic transmissions require ZF-approved fluid. We drain, inspect the pan for metal debris, replace the filter and pan gasket, and refill with the correct spec fluid — restoring smooth shifts.
🔧
Solenoid Replacement
Faulty shift solenoids are a common cause of harsh or delayed shifts in BMW ZF automatics. We test individual solenoids and replace only what's failed — no unnecessary parts.
⚙️
DCT / SMG Service
BMW's M-DCT and older SMG transmissions have unique fluid and clutch wear patterns. We service these performance gearboxes with the correct spec fluid and check actuator calibration.
🔁
Valve Body Repair
The valve body controls hydraulic pressure throughout the automatic transmission. We rebuild or replace valve bodies as needed on ZF 6HP and 8HP units.
🏗️
Full Transmission Rebuild
When internal damage is extensive, a rebuild is often more cost-effective than replacement. We rebuild BMW transmissions to factory spec using OEM or OEM-equivalent components.
