Common Questions

AUDI OIL CHANGE FAQ

How often does an Audi need an oil change?

Audi's oil life monitor will notify you when an oil change is due — typically every 10,000 miles under normal conditions. For turbocharged engines and S/RS performance models, we recommend a 7,500-mile interval to better protect against oil degradation, carbon buildup, and timing chain wear on high-stress engines.

What oil does my Audi need?

Most Audis require full synthetic oil meeting VW 502.00, 504.00, or 508.00 specification depending on engine and model year — typically 0W-20 or 5W-30. S and RS engines may require a specific higher-performance spec. We confirm the correct oil for your exact VIN before we start.

Do I need to go to an Audi dealer for oil changes?

No. Any shop with Audi-trained technicians, VCDS tools to reset the oil life monitor, and VW-spec oil can properly service your Audi — for significantly less than dealer pricing. That's exactly what we offer at German Performance.

Will the oil change reset my service reminder?

Yes — but only with a proper OLM reset using VCDS or compatible software. We reset the oil life monitor on every Audi oil service as standard. A manual button reset through the dash only clears the display — it doesn't properly update the service history in the car's computer.

Do you service all Audi models?

Yes. We service the full Audi lineup including A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q8, TT, R8, and the full S and RS performance range.

How long does an Audi oil change take?

A standard Audi oil change with multi-point inspection and OLM reset typically takes 45 minutes to an hour at our shop.
