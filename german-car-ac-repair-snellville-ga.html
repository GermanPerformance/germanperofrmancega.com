What You Get

GERMAN CAR AC
SERVICES WE OFFER

We handle every level of AC repair — from a simple recharge to full system replacement.

❄️
AC System Diagnostic
We perform a full AC system diagnostic including pressure testing, temperature output measurement, and a scan for HVAC fault codes — identifying the root cause before recommending any repair.
💨
Refrigerant Recharge
We evacuate and recharge your AC system with the correct refrigerant spec for your vehicle — R134a or the newer R1234yf used in most post-2017 European vehicles. We never just top off a leaking system.
🔍
Leak Detection & Repair
AC refrigerant leaks are found using UV dye and electronic leak detectors. We locate leaks at the compressor, condenser, evaporator, hoses, and O-rings — then repair them properly so the charge holds.
⚙️
Compressor Replacement
A seized or failing AC compressor is the most common major AC repair. We replace compressors with OEM or OEM-equivalent units and flush the system to remove metal debris before recharging.
🌬️
Condenser & Evaporator Service
A damaged condenser (from road debris) or a leaking evaporator core requires careful diagnosis. We handle both — including the labor-intensive evaporator replacement that requires dashboard removal on many German vehicles.
🎛️
HVAC Control & Blend Door Repair
Dual-zone climate control issues, blend door actuator failures, and blower motor problems are common on aging German vehicles. We diagnose and repair HVAC control systems with the correct diagnostic software for each brand.
