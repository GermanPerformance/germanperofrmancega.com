WHAT'S INCLUDED IN YOUR
PORSCHE INSPECTION

Every Porsche inspection covers the full vehicle — mechanical, electrical, and structural — with a written report you can use to negotiate, plan maintenance, or simply drive with confidence.

01
PIWIS Diagnostic Scan
We connect Porsche-compatible PIWIS diagnostic software to read all module fault codes — engine, transmission, PDK, PASM, PSM, and more. Every stored and pending code is logged and explained.
02
Full Mechanical Inspection
Engine, transmission, cooling system, brakes, suspension, steering, exhaust, and drivetrain — we inspect every mechanical system and document condition, wear, and any immediate concerns.
03
Brake & Suspension Check
We measure brake pad and rotor thickness, inspect caliper condition, check PCCB ceramic brake wear (if equipped), and inspect all suspension bushings, ball joints, and tie rod ends for wear and play.
04
Fluid Condition & Leak Check
We check engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, transmission fluid, and power steering fluid condition and levels. We also inspect for active leaks at all gaskets, seals, and hose connections.
05
Electrical & IMS/RMS Check
We inspect battery health, charging system, and all lighting. On applicable 996/997 models we check for IMS bearing and RMS seal issues — the known failure points every Porsche buyer needs to know about.
06
Written Inspection Report
You receive a detailed written report covering every system inspected, its condition rating, and recommended actions — ranked by urgency so you know what to address immediately vs. monitor over time.

Common Questions

PORSCHE INSPECTION FAQ

What is a pre-purchase Porsche inspection?

A pre-purchase inspection (PPI) is a comprehensive mechanical and diagnostic evaluation performed before you buy a used Porsche. We check every system, pull fault codes, inspect for known failure points like IMS bearings and RMS seals, and give you a written report — so you know exactly what you're getting before you hand over money.

How much does a Porsche inspection cost?

Call us for current pricing — it varies by model and inspection type. A pre-purchase inspection is one of the best investments you can make on a used Porsche. A single overlooked IMS bearing failure or coolant system issue can cost more than the inspection fee many times over.

What Porsche models do you inspect?

We inspect the full Porsche lineup including 911 (996, 997, 991, 992), Boxster, Cayman, Cayenne, Macan, and Panamera. We have specific knowledge of the platform-specific failure points for each generation.

What is the IMS bearing issue on Porsche 996/997?

The IMS (Intermediate Shaft) bearing on M96 and M97 engines in 996 and early 997 models is a known failure point. When it fails, it can send metal debris through the engine, causing catastrophic damage. We assess IMS bearing risk and condition as part of every inspection on these models and can advise on preventive replacement options.

Can you inspect a Porsche I'm thinking of buying from a private seller?

Yes — this is one of the most common reasons people bring a Porsche to us. Have the seller bring the car to our shop, or arrange transport, and we'll give you a full pre-purchase inspection report. We can also advise on how to use the findings in price negotiations.

How long does a Porsche inspection take?

A comprehensive Porsche inspection typically takes 2–3 hours. We recommend dropping the vehicle off in the morning. We'll call you with findings before you pick it up so you have time to ask questions.
