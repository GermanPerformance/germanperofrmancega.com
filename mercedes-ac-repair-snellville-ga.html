What You Get

MERCEDES AC
SERVICES WE OFFER

We handle every level of Mercedes AC repair — from a simple recharge to a full evaporator core replacement.

01
AC System Diagnostic
We perform a full Mercedes AC diagnostic using STAR-compatible software — reading HVAC fault codes, checking compressor clutch operation, measuring pressure readings, and testing temperature output to identify the root cause before recommending any repair.
02
Refrigerant Recharge
We evacuate and recharge your Mercedes AC system with the correct refrigerant — R134a for older models, R1234yf for most post-2017 Mercedes vehicles. We never top off a leaking system without finding and fixing the leak first.
03
Leak Detection & Repair
Mercedes AC leaks are located using UV dye and electronic leak detectors. Common leak points include the compressor shaft seal, condenser, evaporator, and O-rings at the service ports. We repair leaks properly before recharging the system.
04
Compressor Replacement
A failed Mercedes AC compressor is the most common major AC repair. We replace with OEM or OEM-equivalent compressors, flush the system to remove metal debris, replace the receiver-drier, and recharge with the correct refrigerant spec.
05
Condenser & Evaporator Service
Road debris damage to the condenser and evaporator core leaks are common Mercedes AC failures. Evaporator replacement on many Mercedes models requires dashboard removal — we have the experience to handle the job correctly without damaging trim or wiring.
06
HVAC Control & Blend Door Repair
Mercedes dual-zone COMAND climate control issues, blend door actuator failures, and blower motor problems are diagnosed with STAR software — not guesswork. We identify the specific failed component before recommending any parts.

Common Questions

MERCEDES AC REPAIR FAQ

Why is my Mercedes AC not blowing cold?

The most common causes are low refrigerant from a leak, a failed AC compressor, a damaged condenser, or a faulty expansion valve. Mercedes dual-zone COMAND climate systems can also fail due to blend door actuator issues or HVAC control module faults. We use STAR-compatible software to diagnose the root cause — not guess and replace parts.

What refrigerant does my Mercedes use?

Most Mercedes-Benz vehicles built before 2017 use R134a refrigerant. Most 2017 and newer Mercedes use R1234yf — which requires different equipment and costs more per pound. We confirm the correct refrigerant spec for your specific model before any AC work begins and have the certified equipment to handle both types.

Can you just recharge my Mercedes AC without fixing the leak?

We won't. Recharging a leaking system without fixing the leak is a waste of money — the refrigerant will escape again, usually within a few weeks. We locate and repair the leak first, then recharge. This is the only approach that actually solves the problem long-term.

My Mercedes AC works sometimes but not always — what's causing it?

Intermittent Mercedes AC is usually caused by a failing compressor clutch, a pressure sensor fault, or an HVAC control module issue. Mercedes dual-zone climate systems are complex — the COMAND controller, two blend door actuators, and multiple temperature sensors can all cause intermittent behavior. STAR-compatible diagnostics let us read exactly which component is faulting.

How much does Mercedes AC repair cost?

It depends entirely on what has failed. A refrigerant recharge after a minor O-ring leak is relatively inexpensive. A compressor replacement is more significant, and an evaporator core job — which requires dashboard removal on most Mercedes models — is a major repair. We give you a full written estimate before starting any work.

Do you service AC on all Mercedes models?

Yes. We service AC systems on the full Mercedes-Benz lineup including C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, GLC, GLE, GLS, CLA, CLS, AMG models, and older W124/W210/W211 classics. Each generation has its own HVAC system quirks — we have the tools and experience for all of them.
