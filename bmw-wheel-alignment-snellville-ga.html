What You Get

BMW WHEEL ALIGNMENT
WHAT'S INCLUDED

A proper BMW alignment goes beyond a basic toe adjustment — we set every alignment angle to factory spec and verify the result.

🔍
Pre-Alignment Inspection
Before we align your BMW, we inspect all suspension and steering components on the lift — control arms, tie rod ends, ball joints, and wheel bearings. Aligning a car with worn suspension components is pointless; the alignment will shift immediately.
📐
4-Wheel Alignment
We perform a full 4-wheel alignment — measuring and adjusting camber, caster, and toe on all four corners to BMW factory specifications. A 2-wheel front-only alignment misses the rear geometry that affects straight-line tracking and tire wear.
🏎️
M Sport & Lowered Vehicle Alignment
BMW M Sport suspension and aftermarket lowering springs require alignment adjustments that fall outside standard spec ranges. We have the knowledge and equipment to properly align M models and modified BMWs to achieve the correct geometry for their setup.
🔧
Steering Angle Sensor Reset
After a wheel alignment, many BMW models require a steering angle sensor reset using BMW ISTA software. Without it, the DSC and lane departure warning systems may display faults. We perform this reset as standard after every alignment.
🛞
Tire Condition Assessment
During the alignment, we measure tread depth on all four tires and inspect for uneven wear patterns that reveal suspension or alignment history. We let you know if existing tire wear is too far gone to recover from alignment alone.
📋
Before & After Alignment Report
You receive a printed before-and-after alignment report showing every angle measured and the adjustments made — so you have documentation of the work performed and can verify the car is back to spec.
