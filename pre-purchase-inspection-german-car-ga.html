What You Get

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN YOUR
PRE-PURCHASE INSPECTION

Our pre-purchase inspection covers every system a buyer needs to know about — mechanical, electrical, structural, and cosmetic.

01
Full Diagnostic Scan
We scan every module — engine, transmission, ABS, airbags, suspension, and more — using brand-specific diagnostic software. Stored fault codes that have been cleared show up here. A seller can clear codes; they can't erase the history.
02
Full Mechanical Inspection
Engine, transmission, cooling system, brakes, suspension, steering, exhaust, and drivetrain — every mechanical system is inspected on the lift and documented with condition ratings.
03
Fluid Condition Check
We check oil condition and level, coolant condition, brake fluid moisture content, transmission fluid, and power steering fluid. Neglected fluids tell a story about how the car was maintained.
04
Tire & Brake Assessment
We measure brake pad thickness, rotor condition, and tire tread depth. We also check for uneven tire wear — a tell-tale sign of alignment issues, suspension wear, or a history of hard driving.
05
Known Failure Point Check
Every German car platform has known failure points. We check for BMW IMS bearing risk, VW timing chain rattle, Audi Haldex condition, Mercedes coolant system issues, and Porsche-specific concerns based on the exact model in front of us.
06
Written Report with Repair Estimates
You receive a written report with every finding, condition ratings, and estimated repair costs for any issues found — so you can negotiate the purchase price, walk away, or budget for upcoming maintenance with full information.

WHY GET A
PRE-PURCHASE INSPECTION?

Used German cars can be incredible values — or expensive traps. The difference is almost always what the previous owner didn't tell you. A pre-purchase inspection gives you the full picture before you sign anything.

  • Brand-specific diagnostics for BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Porsche, and VW
  • Platform-specific failure point checks — we know what to look for on every model
  • Cleared fault codes leave a history — our scan reveals what sellers tried to hide
  • Written report with repair cost estimates for negotiating the purchase price
  • We inspect cars brought by private sellers, dealers, and auction buyers
  • One inspection fee can save you thousands on a bad purchase

PRE-PURCHASE INSPECTION FAQ

Call us to schedule. If you're buying from a private seller, ask them to drive the car to our shop or arrange transport. If buying from a dealer, most reputable dealers will allow an independent inspection — if they refuse, that itself is a red flag. We'll inspect the car while you wait or give you a full report the same day.

Call us for current pricing — it varies slightly by model. A pre-purchase inspection is one of the best investments you can make on a used German car. A single missed timing chain issue, IMS bearing problem, or hidden accident damage can cost far more than the inspection fee to repair.

We inspect BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen, and Porsche. Each brand requires different diagnostic software and platform-specific knowledge — a generic inspection misses brand-specific failure points that can cost thousands to repair.

We look for signs of previous body work, mismatched panel gaps, overspray, and structural issues. While we're not a dedicated body shop, our technicians know what repaired collision damage looks like and we document anything suspicious. We also recommend obtaining a CARFAX report before purchase for the full ownership and accident history.
