What You Get

WHAT WE
REPAIR

A German car tells you what is wrong in more detail than most, if the tools can read it. Every repair here starts by reading the car properly, and ends with the fault gone rather than the light off.

01

Factory-Level Diagnostics

ISTA for BMW, XENTRY for Mercedes-Benz, ODIS for Audi and Volkswagen, PIWIS for Porsche. A generic scanner reads the engine module and little else; the factory software reads every control unit on the car, with the live data and the guided tests that come with it.
02

Engine Repair

Oil leaks from valve cover, oil filter housing and timing cover gaskets, timing chain guides, PCV valves, water pumps, high-pressure fuel pumps and injectors. The failures on each engine family are well documented, and so are the right parts to fix them.
03

Electrical and Module Faults

Battery and charging faults, wiring and connector damage, failed control modules and the coding and programming that follow a replacement. A new module on a modern German car is not working until it is coded to the vehicle.
04

Cooling System

Plastic radiators, expansion tanks, thermostats and electric water pumps age on a schedule. We pressure-test the system, find the leak rather than guess at it, and replace what has failed with OEM or OEM-equivalent parts.
05

Suspension and Steering

Control arm bushings, ball joints, tie rods, shocks, struts and air suspension components, then a four-wheel alignment to the factory specification once the parts are in. Worn bushings are the most common cause of the clunks and wander owners report.
06

Drivetrain and Differential

Driveshaft center bearings and flex discs, CV axles and boots, differential and transfer case fluid services and seals. Fluids the manufacturer once called lifetime still wear, and the units they protect are expensive to replace.

Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

What German car brands do you service?

German Performance works only on German-engineered vehicles: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche and Volkswagen, including MINI, AMG, BMW M, Audi Sport and the Sprinter. Our technicians are licensed and certified, trained by certified BMW and Mercedes-Benz mechanics, and use each marque's factory diagnostic software and OEM parts, giving you dealership-level expertise at independent shop pricing.

Where is German Performance located and what are your hours?

We're located at 2144 Parkwood Rd NW, Snellville, GA 30078, serving the greater Gwinnett County area including Lawrenceville, Grayson, Loganville, and Stone Mountain. Call us at (678) 395-7459 to confirm current hours and schedule your appointment. Same-week slots are usually available.

Do you use OEM parts or aftermarket parts?

We primarily use OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and OEM-equivalent parts to maintain the performance, safety, and warranty integrity of your vehicle. For older models or budget-conscious repairs, we'll discuss quality aftermarket alternatives with you, always with full transparency on what's going in your car and why.

Will servicing my car here void my dealership warranty?

No. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, dealers cannot void your factory warranty simply because you chose an independent shop for routine maintenance and repairs, provided the work is performed correctly using appropriate parts. German Performance documents all services thoroughly, so your records are always dealer-presentation ready. We wrote up what actually changes when you leave the dealer if you want the longer answer.

How long have you been in business and are you CARFAX-rated?

German Performance has been serving Gwinnett County since 2010, over a decade of specializing in German vehicles. CARFAX named us a 2025 Top-Rated Service Center, which means our service records are reported to CARFAX and our quality is independently verified through customer reviews and complaint history.

What diagnostic equipment do you use?

We use factory-level diagnostic tools, the same systems the dealerships use, including ISTA for BMW, XENTRY/DAS for Mercedes-Benz, ODIS for Audi/VW, and PIWIS for Porsche. This means we can read all control modules, access live data, perform adaptations, and code new components, not just clear codes with a generic OBD-II scanner.

How do I schedule an appointment?

The easiest way is to call us directly at (678) 395-7459. Same-week appointments are usually available. When you call, have your vehicle's year, make, model, and a brief description of the issue handy so we can estimate the time needed.

How do you estimate a repair?

Diagnosis first, then a written estimate that lists the parts and the labor before anything is ordered. If something changes once the car is apart, you get a call and a choice, not a surprise on the invoice.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Every repair we perform carries a 12-month, 12,000-mile warranty. Keep the invoice: it is the record the warranty runs on, and the one a future buyer will ask to see.

Do you work with extended warranties?

Yes. We work with extended warranties and accept most warranty companies, including CarShield, Endurance, Allstate, Route 66, CARCHEX and American Auto Shield. Bring your coverage information when you come in and we'll handle the claims process directly with your provider.
