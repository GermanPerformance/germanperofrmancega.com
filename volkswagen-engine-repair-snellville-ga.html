What You Get

VW ENGINE
SERVICES

From a targeted repair to a full engine overhaul — we handle every level of VW engine work.

🔍
Engine Diagnostic Scan
We use VCDS (Ross-Tech) and OEM-level VW diagnostic software to pull fault codes, live sensor data, and adaptation values — giving us an accurate picture before any parts are touched.
🔗
Timing Chain Service
Complete EA888 timing chain kit replacement including chain, tensioner, guides, and cam phasers. We don't cut corners — the full kit goes in so you're not back in 20,000 miles for the piece we skipped.
🌰
Walnut Blasting (Carbon Cleaning)
We remove the intake manifold and walnut-blast the intake valves to remove carbon deposits — restoring power, smoothing idle, and eliminating misfires caused by direct injection carbon buildup.
💧
Oil Leak Repair
We trace oil leaks to their source using UV dye and pressure testing — cam cover, rear main seal, oil cooler, or oil pan — and repair them properly with OEM gaskets and seals.
🌀
Turbocharger Replacement
We replace failed or failing turbos with OEM or quality aftermarket units, address the underlying cause (oil starvation, boost leaks, PCV failure), and verify boost pressure post-install.
🏗️
Engine Rebuild & Replacement
When engine damage is extensive — spun bearings, seized pistons — we assess whether a rebuild or replacement makes more sense and give you an honest recommendation.
